Why it matters

Your personality is not one skill.

It is how you dress, walk, sit, speak, listen and lead, all working together. People read all of it at once, long before they read your CV.

Small habits hold capable people back in interviews, in meetings and in front of clients. We look at the whole picture, pick the few changes that make the biggest difference, and build them into a short practical course.

What we work on

Everything that shapes how people see you.

How you look

  • Dressing right for your role and the occasion
  • Grooming and a neat, polished appearance
  • An upright, relaxed posture
  • How you sit, stand and walk into a room

How you sound

  • The tone, pitch and pace of your voice
  • Knowing when to be gentle and when to be firm
  • Speaking clearly so every word lands
  • Choosing the right words for the moment

How you come across

  • Your energy and enthusiasm
  • Eye contact, a genuine smile and body language
  • Strong first impressions and good manners
  • Listening so people feel heard

How you lead

  • Taking responsibility and owning results
  • Making decisions with confidence
  • Managing people, time and priorities
  • Staying calm and composed under pressure

How the course works

See it. Focus it. Practise it. Use it on Monday.

  1. See yourself clearly

    We start with how you come across today — on video, in a conversation, walking into a room — so you can see what everyone else already sees.

  2. Focus on what matters

    Not every habit is worth changing. We pick the few that make the biggest difference to how you are read at work, and leave the rest alone.

  3. Practise for real

    Role-plays, mock meetings and video feedback, guided by experienced coaches. You practise the difficult moments here, where getting it wrong costs nothing.

  4. Apply it at work

    You leave with the habits, the words and a plan for the next meeting, interview or client conversation in your diary.
