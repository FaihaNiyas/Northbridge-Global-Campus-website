Work with us.

We are not advertising named vacancies at the moment. What we are doing is building a list of people we want to call first — and that list is genuinely how we hire.

How we engage people

Mostly course by course, not desk by desk.

Most of the people who teach at Northbridge keep their day jobs. We engage them for a specific course or a run of sessions, agree the scope and the fee up front, and pay for the work delivered. It suits practitioners who want to teach without giving up practice.

A small core team runs operations, admissions and growth. Those roles are permanent, and we fill them rarely and carefully.

Course leads and tutors

Practitioners in commercial finance, workplace English, digital and AI skills, spreadsheets and reporting, and sales. You should be doing the work, not only teaching it.

Content and assessment

People who can turn expertise into exercises, worked examples and assessments that test capability rather than recall.

Operations and growth

Admissions, learner support, marketing and partnerships. Broad roles in a small team, with a lot of ownership.

Industry partners

Employers who want a team trained on something specific, and are willing to help shape what gets taught.
