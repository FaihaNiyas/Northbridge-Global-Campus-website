Ways to work with us

Four ways in. Start wherever it fits.

Teach a programme

Deliver one programme, or one session of one, without stepping away from your practice. We handle the platform, the learners and the admin — you bring the expertise.

Build a long-term collaboration

Join us repeatedly, own a subject area, shape how it is taught as the academy grows.

Design the learning

Help build programmes, cases, exercises and assessments. Ideal if you would rather design than present.

Lead corporate work

Deliver practical capability building inside client organisations, with Northbridge bringing the relationship and the structure.

What you get from it: engagements that fit around your practice, a visible platform under your own name, a role in shaping a new Sri Lankan institution from the beginning — and the professional pull that comes from being known as the person who teaches this.

What we look for

We are not looking for the best presenter. We are looking for the best practitioner who can explain.

People who know their subject from having done it. Who can make a hard idea simple without making it wrong. Who bring real cases, real numbers, real mistakes. Who push learners to think rather than to transcribe. And who genuinely care whether a learner can use the thing afterwards.

How we design a programme with you

We do not start from a syllabus. We start from one question: what should the learner be able to do when this is over? Then we work backwards.

It changes everything about how a session is built. Do not explain negotiation — put learners in one. Do not describe a dashboard — have them build it. Do not talk about cash flow — hand them a company that is about to run out of it and ask what they would do by Friday.

Bring the real world in with you: real companies, real cases, real problems, real decisions, real lessons learned the hard way. That is the part learners cannot get from a book, and it is the part they will remember.
