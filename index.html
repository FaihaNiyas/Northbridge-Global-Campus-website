The launch programmes

Five programmes. Five things you will be able to do by the end.

Each one is short, online, practitioner-led and built around a real output.

  • 01 · Accounting & Finance

    FinBridge™

    Don’t just qualify. Become effective.

    For people who have passed their accounting exams: faster month-ends, and numbers turned into advice the business can act on.

    You finish with: a faster month-end routine of your own, rebuilt step by step, and a short set of recommendations you could take to a manager.

  • 02 · Communication & Confidence

    SpeakSmart

    Know what to say. Know how to say it. Be heard.

    For capable professionals who hesitate in meetings, presentations and interviews. We practise the real moments until speaking up feels natural.

    You finish with: a delivered presentation, a rewritten set of your own work emails, and a plan for the conversations you have been avoiding.

  • 03 · AI & Smarter Work

    WorkSmart with AI

    Use AI to work more efficiently, and know when to check its output.

    Practical AI for everyday office work, practised on your own tasks, including where the tools are confidently wrong.

    You finish with: two workflows from your own job, rebuilt and timed before and after.

  • 04 · Data & Performance

    Excel to Dashboard

    From a sheet nobody trusts to a report a decision can be made from.

    Clean the data, build the logic, then build the view that lets a busy person decide in thirty seconds.

    You finish with: a working dashboard, built from real data, that you can take straight to work.

  • 05 · Sales & Customer Growth

    Digital Sales Engine

    Turn attention into enquiries, and enquiries into paying customers.

    Offer, message, ads, WhatsApp and follow-up, plus the numbers that show which part is leaking.

    You finish with: a live funnel for your own business or a case business, and the dashboard to measure it.

Straight answers

The questions you are probably about to ask.

Are you accredited?

Not yet. Northbridge Global Campus (Pvt) Ltd is a registered Sri Lankan company, and we are not currently accredited by an awarding body. When that changes, we will name the body here.

What do I actually get?

A Northbridge certificate of completion, and a real output you can show people, such as a dashboard, a snapshot or a rebuilt workflow.

Who teaches?

Practising professionals, engaged programme by programme. Lecturer details are published for each programme before enrolment opens.

Is it online? How much time will it take?

Online, in short programmes designed around a working week. Exact schedules are published with each programme.

Do I need a finance or technical background?

Not for Excel to Dashboard, WorkSmart with AI or SpeakSmart. FinBridge™ is for people who have already studied accounting.

When can I enrol?

Enrolments are not open yet, and we are not quoting fees or dates until they are settled.
