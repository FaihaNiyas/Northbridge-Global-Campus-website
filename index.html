What we are building

Courses that change what you can do on Monday morning.

Plenty of courses hand you a certificate. Ours are designed so that the week after you finish, you can do something at work you could not do before. That is the whole test we set ourselves.

  1. First

    Short online courses

    Focused programmes you can complete alongside a full-time job, taught in the evenings and at weekends.

  2. Then

    A working academy

    A wider set of connected programmes, with progression from one level to the next rather than a scatter of unrelated certificates.

  3. In time

    A campus with global reach

    Sri Lankan roots, international standards, and learners who can work with organisations anywhere.

Where we are starting

The first subject areas.

These are the areas we are preparing first. We are not taking enrolments yet — when dates and fees are confirmed, they will be published here and nowhere else first.

Commercial finance for non-accountants

Reading the numbers, understanding margins and cash, and holding your own in a budget conversation — for managers who were never trained in it.

Workplace English

The English you actually need at work: emails that get replies, meetings you can speak up in, and interviews you walk out of feeling steady.

Working with AI

Using AI tools sensibly in ordinary office work — drafting, summarising, checking — and knowing where they should not be trusted.

Spreadsheets and reporting

Getting from a messy sheet to a report someone can make a decision from, without needing a data team.

Who we are

A young institution, and we say so plainly.

We would rather tell you exactly where we stand than dress it up. Here is what is true today.

  • Northbridge Global Campus (Pvt) Ltd is a company incorporated in Sri Lanka.
  • We are building our first programmes now. Enrolments are not open yet.
  • Teaching is delivered by practitioners engaged for each course, not by a permanent faculty we do not yet have.
  • We hold our own domains, accounts and learner records, so nothing about your data sits with a third party we cannot answer for.
  • Any recognition or accreditation we obtain will be named on this site, with the awarding body, when it exists.
