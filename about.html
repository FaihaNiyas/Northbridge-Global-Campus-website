About Northbridge

A modern learning company focused on what people can actually do with what they learn.

Why we exist

A qualification matters. Capability matters too.

Northbridge focuses on the gap between knowing something and being able to use it confidently in real situations. Our programmes are designed around practical work, real examples and useful outcomes.

How we work

Four commitments.

Practical by design

Learning should be useful, not just informative.

Taught by people who understand the work

Bring practitioners and real-world experience into the learning experience.

Built for real people

Learning should fit around work, business, study and life.

Focused on real improvement

Participants should leave with greater capability, confidence and something they can actually use.

Our name

A bridge to where you want to go.

Northbridge represents connection, progress and reach—helping people move from where they are today toward where they want to go. The globe reflects our wider ambition; the cap represents learning and achievement.

Where we stand today

The plain facts.

  • Northbridge Global Campus (Pvt) Ltd is incorporated in Sri Lanka.
  • We are preparing our first programmes. Enrolments are not open, and we are not quoting fees or dates until they are settled.
  • We do not currently hold accreditation from an awarding body. If and when we do, it will be named here alongside the body that granted it.
  • We are a small core team, working with specialists course by course.
