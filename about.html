About Northbridge.

A Sri Lankan company building education that is judged by one thing: whether it changes what our learners can do at work.

Learn · Build · Lead · Go Global

Why we exist

The gap between a qualification and a capability.

Sri Lanka does not have a shortage of qualifications. What it has is a gap between holding a certificate and being able to do the work — reading a set of accounts, chairing a meeting in English, turning a spreadsheet into something a director can act on.

Northbridge exists to close that gap. Every course we build starts from a real task somebody is expected to perform at work, and works backwards to what has to be taught.

How we work

Four commitments.

Taught by people who do the work

Our teaching is delivered by practitioners — accountants, managers, specialists — engaged for a specific course. They bring what the job is like this year, not what a syllabus said a decade ago.

Built for people with jobs

Courses are short, online first, and scheduled around evenings and weekends. You should not have to leave your work to get better at it.

Honest about what we are

We will not describe ourselves as bigger, older or more accredited than we are. If we do not yet have something, we will say so on this website.

Careful with your information

We hold our own domains, systems and learner records. Your details are used to answer your enquiry and nothing else, and we do not sell them.

Our name

A bridge, a globe, a graduation cap.

The crest is quartered. The N for Northbridge. A suspension bridge, because our job is to carry people from where they are to where they want to work. A globe, for the standard we hold ourselves to and the reach we want for our learners. And a graduation cap, for the learning itself.

The motto reads Discere · Conectere · Excellere — to learn, to connect, to excel. It is the sequence we believe careers actually follow.

Where we stand today

The plain facts.

  • Northbridge Global Campus (Pvt) Ltd is incorporated in Sri Lanka.
  • We are preparing our first programmes. Enrolments are not open, and we are not quoting fees or dates until they are settled.
  • We do not currently hold accreditation from an awarding body. If and when we do, it will be named here alongside the body that granted it.
  • We are a small core team, working with specialists course by course.
