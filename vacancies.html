The role

Practitioners who can teach what they know.

Our key requirements

  • Relevant professional qualifications and/or strong industry credentials in your subject
  • Sound practical industry knowledge and professional experience
  • Teaching or training experience preferred, with good communication and presentation skills
  • A genuine passion for teaching, mentoring and developing professional capabilities
  • The ability to deliver engaging, interactive online sessions

Why join Northbridge

  • 100% online: teach from anywhere in the world
  • A flexible revenue-sharing model
  • Turn your real-world experience into practical learning
  • Help professionals build skills they can use at work
  • Be part of a new, practitioner-led way of learning
  • Join our Founding Practitioner Network

How to apply

Send your CV. Tell us what you could teach.

  1. Email your CV

    Send your updated CV to hr@northbridgeglobalcampus.com and mention your preferred programme or programmes.

  2. Use a clear subject line

    Lecturer Application: [Programme name], for example “Lecturer Application: SpeakSmart”.

  3. We review every application

    Our team reviews all applications and contacts shortlisted candidates.

  4. Send a 10-minute teaching video

    Shortlisted candidates are asked for a 10-minute teaching demonstration video showing their subject expertise, practical teaching approach and presentation skills.
